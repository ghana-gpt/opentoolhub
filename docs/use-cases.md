# Use cases and examples

OpenToolHub is most useful when we organize tools around what people want to build.

## Example categories

### Travel and city life

- weather APIs
- maps and geolocation
- local event data
- transportation systems
- currency conversion
- hotel and attraction data

### Public data and civic systems

- government and open data APIs
- local service directories
- environmental data
- public safety and alerts
- transparency dashboards

### AI workflows

- search and retrieval
- document summarization
- translation and language tools
- vector and knowledge systems
- AI agent tool integrations

### Startup and product ideas

- local business search
- job dashboards
- market intelligence tools
- finance and portfolio trackers
- data visualization projects

### Nonprofit and community tools

- public health reporting
- donation and impact dashboards
- volunteer coordination tools
- education support platforms
- issue and resource tracking
