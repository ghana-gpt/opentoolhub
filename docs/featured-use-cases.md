# Featured use cases

This document highlights the first core product directions for OpenToolHub.

## 1. Travel and local discovery

Use this when the product needs local context, destination information, or trip planning support.

Typical tools:

- weather feeds
- maps and geolocation services
- routing and travel data
- calendar or local event feeds
- local business or attraction datasets

## 2. City dashboards and local insight tools

Use this when the product needs a simple overview of local conditions or community information.

Typical tools:

- map layers
- events or city feed data
- public dataset sources
- weather or environmental feeds
- local alert information

## 3. Community and public-health tools

Use this when the product needs health, public-interest, or local reporting workflows.

Typical tools:

- health datasets
- public datasets
- local maps
- environmental data
- reports and status feeds

## 4. AI-powered research and assistants

Use this when the product needs to gather information and present it clearly.

Typical tools:

- search and retrieval
- public knowledge sources
- language or translation tools
- summary or reasoning support
- orchestration across multiple sources

## 5. Startup MVPs and prototypes

Use this when the goal is to validate an idea quickly using public tools rather than building everything from scratch.

Typical tools:

- maps
- local discovery
- finance or market data
- open public datasets
- analytics and feedback collection

## Why these use cases matter

They are practical, understandable, and easy to turn into starter examples.

This makes the project easier to contribute to, easier to explain, and easier to act on.
