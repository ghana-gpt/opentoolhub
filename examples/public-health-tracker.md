# Public health tracker example

## Product idea

A community-focused tracking tool that presents local health conditions, public reports, and alerts in a simple, understandable interface.

## Problem it solves

People need a clear way to understand public health information, environmental conditions, and local updates without navigating multiple fragmented sources.

## Suggested stack

- health or public data feeds
- map and location services
- weather or environment data
- alert or reporting APIs
- dashboard or reporting interface

## Feature ideas

- local health summary cards
- map-based condition views
- alert feed for major updates
- comparison between communities or regions
- simple reporting or public information sharing

## Example user flow

1. User picks a community or region.
2. Relevant health and environmental information loads.
3. Alerts and public updates are highlighted.
4. The user can inspect local conditions and trends.
5. The summary helps inform decisions or actions.

## Why this matters

This kind of project shows how public-interest tools can combine real datasets with useful communication patterns to support communities.
